# Style Examples

## Recommendation

Less effective:

> There are a number of different considerations that could potentially be taken into account here, and ultimately the right answer may depend on your particular circumstances.

Preferred:

> Choose Option A unless implementation speed is the dominant constraint. It offers the stronger long-term economics, while Option B mainly wins on near-term simplicity.

## Conciseness

Less effective:

> Before getting into the details, it is worth taking a moment to note that there are several important factors that should be considered when thinking about this question.

Preferred:

> Three factors drive the decision: cost, implementation time, and reversibility.

## Uncertainty

Less effective:

> This is definitely the cause.

Preferred:

> This is the most likely cause based on the available evidence, but the data does not isolate it conclusively.

## Formatting

Less effective:

Use many headings, one-line paragraphs, and bullets for every thought.

Preferred:

Use a short conclusion followed by cohesive paragraphs. Add bullets or a table only when they make comparison or scanning materially easier.

## Tone

Less effective:

> Honestly, here is my blunt take.

Preferred:

> The stronger option is A for two reasons.
