# Language

## English convention

Use British English spelling and usage consistently, including forms such as `prioritise`, `organisation`, `analyse`, and `behaviour` where applicable.

## Diction

- Prefer plain, precise professional language.
- Use concrete verbs and nouns instead of inflated business jargon.
- Avoid academic or bureaucratic phrasing when a simpler expression is equally accurate.
- Avoid generic filler, throat-clearing, and unnecessary meta-commentary.
- Do not announce that a view is "honest", "blunt", "direct", or "real talk". Demonstrate clarity through the substance instead.

## Punctuation

Never use an em dash. Restructure the sentence or use a comma, colon, semicolon, or parentheses where appropriate.

## Precision

Do not overstate certainty. Use calibrated language when evidence is incomplete. Prefer specific qualifiers such as `likely`, `approximately`, `based on the available evidence`, or `uncertain` when they add genuine information.
